How I got here

I'm Tony, a product manager who grew up in Sleepy Hollow, NY (yes, that one) that can't stop building things.

I've been taking things apart, figuring out how they work, and building my own for as long as I can remember. Professionally, though, I took a detour: eight years trading FX options at a bank.

I loved it. To me, the currency market was a giant, constantly moving puzzle about whether countries could solve their problems, and every trade was a bet on the answer. You formed a view, put money behind it, and found out pretty quickly whether you were right.

But on nights and weekends, I kept building. Eventually, I decided to make it my full-time job.

Today I'm a PM at Microsoft Advertising, where I work on turning new signups into successful advertisers, from onboarding and incentives to the notifications that help them along the way. Before that, I worked on Microsoft Advertising Editor, the desktop app our largest advertisers use to manage campaigns at scale.

After hours, I'm building more than ever. AI hasn't just made it faster to turn ideas into products; it's opened up a whole new universe of problems I can understand, explore, and build for.

The thread through all of it is pretty simple: I like figuring out how things work, identifying problems, and building solutions. That curiosity has taken me from trading floors to startups to Microsoft, and a few other places along the way: an MBA from NYU, a master's from Columbia, a commission as an officer in the Navy Reserve, and the Ironman start line.
